# Provenance

The released negative claims are computed from two public rank-47 decompositions of the 4×4 matrix-multiplication tensor over `F2`.

## AlphaTensor seed

The normalized factor data are stored at `mod4/data/r47_alphatensor_f2_factors.json` and `f3/data/r47_alphatensor_f2_factors.json`.

The source record used during the research campaign was the verified `solven-eu/matmulcatalog` entry for the AlphaTensor rank-47 `F2` scheme.

Normalized source SHA-256 used by the `F3` distance audit:

```text
48cc645eaa0b37cf39fe1f079354e06bb6448f2c837ac965993ec43d292b9ed7
```

## Kauers–Moosbauer seed

The normalized factor data are stored at `mod4/data/r47_flips_data.json` and `f3/data/r47_flips_data.json`.

The source record used during the research campaign was the public Kauers–Moosbauer flip-graph rank-47 mod-2 decomposition, also represented in `solven-eu/matmulcatalog`.

Normalized source SHA-256 used by the `F3` distance audit:

